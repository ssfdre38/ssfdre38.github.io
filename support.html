Research Philosophy

Compete with billion-dollar labs on $100 budgets. DeepMind and OpenAI have massive resources—we have creativity, transparency, and a willingness to test unconventional ideas. Grey Liquid experiments cost server rental for a weekend, not millions in compute.

Document everything. Every experiment gets a detailed report—whether it succeeds or fails. The community learns from both. Failure modes teach more than successes.

Democratize access. If advanced AI only runs on $10,000 GPUs, it's not really accessible. G4 Turbo/Nano prove that powerful models can run on phones and single-board computers. Everyone should be able to participate in the AI revolution.

Current Research Status

Grey Liquid Lab - Q1 2026

Investigating the 3-bit quantization barrier that prevents models from being compressed below ~3.4 bits per weight. Four experiments completed:

  • Experiment #1: Q2_K quantization (2.96 bpw) - compiler alignment failure
  • Experiment #2: Importance matrix analysis - incomplete tensor coverage discovered
  • Experiment #3: GPTQ comparison - confirmed universal 3-bit floor across all methods
  • Experiment #4: Mixed-precision bypass - precision distribution matters more than average

Next: Test non-PLE architectures (Llama 3.3, Phi-4) to isolate whether Gemma 4's PLE pathway is the specific bottleneck, or if this is a universal mathematical floor.
